# Global-Road-Accidents
INTRODUCTION Objective of the Project The objective of this project was to conduct a comprehensive analysis of global road accident patterns using secondary data and validate the observed findings using a primary dataset focused on road crashes in Nigeria. The analysis aimed to identify the major factors contributing to road accidents.
The project also sought to:

Examine accident trends across countries and regions.

Analyze accident occurrences by time, day, and period.

Investigate the influence of speeding, alcohol consumption, drugs, and environmental conditions.

Evaluate accident severity through fatalities, injuries, and casualties.

Assess the relationship between road safety infrastructure and accident occurrence.

Validate global accident findings using Nigeria road crash data.

Develop evidence-based recommendations for policymakers and transportation stakeholders.

Problem Being Addressed
Road traffic accidents remain one of the leading causes of preventable deaths and injuries worldwide. Despite continuous advancements in transportation systems and road safety campaigns, accident rates continue to rise in many regions due to human behavior, poor infrastructure, inadequate enforcement, unsafe driving practices, and environmental conditions.

Globally, road accidents contribute significantly to:

Loss of human lives.

Permanent disabilities and injuries.

Economic losses.

Increased healthcare costs.

Reduced workforce productivity.

Social and psychological trauma.

The problem addressed in this project focuses on understanding the underlying causes and patterns associated with road accidents while identifying measurable relationships between accident occurrence and variables such as:

Overspeeding.

Alcohol and drug influence.

Time of day.

Day of the week.

Driver age groups.

Geographic location.

Crash categories.

Road safety compliance.
